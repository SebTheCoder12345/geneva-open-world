GENEVA · CREDITS & ARCHITECTURE

GENEVA is an interactive WebGL open-world sandbox inspired by CROSSTOWN, built with Three.js and procedural PBR architectural generation for instant loading and locked 60 FPS in any modern browser.

Landmarks Represented

  • Rade de Genève & Jet d'Eau — 140m animated water plume & Jetée des Eaux-Vives
  • Pont du Mont-Blanc & Île Rousseau — Flags, Rhône outflow, and Pont des Bergues
  • Jardin Anglais & Horloge Fleurie — Lakeside park & working Flower Clock
  • Vieille-Ville & Cathédrale Saint-Pierre — Elevated Old Town & twin-tower cathedral
  • Bains des Pâquis — Pier, lighthouse, and lakeside promenade
  • Place des Nations — Palais des Nations & Broken Chair sculpture

Upgrading to Photorealistic Swiss Open Data

The world loader is modular: you can drop in .glb city tiles baked in Blender from swisstopo swissBUILDINGS3D, SITG Geneva Open Data, and OpenStreetMap with KTX2 textures.
